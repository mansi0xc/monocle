Monocle Open inspector

Finality, examined to the last vote.

Monocle is a precise instrument for Alpenglow evidence. It checks each certificate’s BLS signature and exact signer stake against a validator checkpoint you choose, then states what the result establishes and what it leaves to you.

Examine a signed exampleWhat it establishes
  • Fast pathOne certificate, 80 / 100 stake against an 80% threshold
  • Slow pathNotarization and finalization, 65 / 100 each against 60%, never added
  • Genesis anchor82 / 100 against 82%, kept separate from block finality

Every reading comes from Monocle’s signed synthetic fixtures (public test keys, invented stake). They demonstrate the instrument, not the network.

What a result establishes, and what stays with you.

Monocle establishes

  • The aggregate signature verifies for the exact signed bytesBLS12-381, with each selected key’s proof of possession checked
  • The signers carry enough stakeExact integers against the checkpoint’s original denominator
  • Which finality path holds, if anyFast, slow and genesis scopes are reported separately
  • What one changed byte breaksA fresh comparison against the immutable original

You remain responsible for

  • Trusting the validator checkpointMonocle records your reason; it does not authenticate the set
  • Context a raw RPC response lacksRecorded getAgGenesisCert responses stay inconclusive without it
  • Ancestry, execution and epoch transitionsNone of these are evaluated
  • Reading a rejection correctlyRejected evidence is not proof that a block is non-final
