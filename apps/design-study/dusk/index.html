See finality reach the summit.

Monocle verifies Alpenglow certificates in your browser, covering BLS signatures, key proofs, exact signer stake and the fast or slow path, against a validator checkpoint you choose. When evidence falls short, it names the check that stopped it.

Open the inspector See what a run reports

The ridge is real fixture data. Ranks 0, 1 and 3 sign 40 + 25 + 15 of 100 stake and reach the 80% fast-path line exactly. Synthetic keys, invented stake.

Every run ends with a reason.

These are real results from Monocle’s signed fixtures. Each conclusion carries a stable diagnostic code, and a rejection never claims a block is non-final.

    Verify on your machine

    The Rust verifier runs as WebAssembly in a browser worker. Once the page is loaded, evidence never leaves it and no network is needed.

    Checkpoint authenticity stays your assumption, recorded beside the result.

    Change one byte

    Derive an experiment from the immutable original, re-verify both under the same checkpoint, and see the first check that breaks.

    One equal-length replacement per experiment, never stacked.

    Hand someone the replay

    Export original bytes, the mutation and its digests. On import every verdict is recomputed; saved reports grant nothing.

    Trust must be selected again by the recipient.
